[ TIER 4 // PERFORMANCE POOL ]

IP Integrity & Contributor Safeguards

To maintain editorial continuity and protect the studio from bad-faith disruption, our agreements enforce clear IP, embargo, and expense settlement standards:

[ A. STATUTORY IP ASSIGNMENT (COPYRIGHT ACT, 1957) ]

All raw video footage, audio stems, project files (`.prproj`/`.aep`), and rendered media created during engagement are permanently assigned to Rethinker Media from the exact moment of creation under Section 19 of the Copyright Act, 1957. Zero "hostage media" practices are permitted.

[ B. 12-MONTH CONCEPT EMBARGO ]

To protect investigative research and field workflows, unreleased documentary concepts, shot lists, and field leads are bound by a 12-month non-disclosure embargo following any contributor exit.

[ C. EXIT EXPENSE SETTLEMENT BUFFER ]

In the event of a contributor departure, unvested equity lapses instantly to the studio pool. However, all verified, pre-approved Tier-3 out-of-pocket field shoot expenses are guaranteed 100% reimbursement within a 45 to 60-day settlement window following notice, protecting studio liquidity while fulfilling all commitments.

[ D. PORTFOLIO EXCERPT RIGHTS ]

Contributors retain formal authorization to showcase short visual excerpts (15–30 second snippets) in their personal portfolio reels after official public release by the studio.
