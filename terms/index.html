FOUNDING GOVERNANCE & TERMS.

At Rethinker Media, we operate with absolute clarity. This document outlines our structural framework for pre-incorporation equity, milestone vesting schedules, intellectual property rights, and operational discipline.

[ OFFICIAL REGISTRATION POLICY & TIMELINE ]

Rethinker Media operates lean during early principal photography. On Day 1, all founding equity promises are secured via a legally binding Pre-Incorporation Memorandum of Understanding (MoU). Official Private Limited company incorporation is hard-coded into our roadmap for Month 6—synchronized precisely with our first vesting cliff and initial equity issuance.

01 // EQUITY POOL & CAP TABLE STRUCTURE

02 // VESTING SCHEDULE & CORPORATE MILESTONES

Pre-Incorporation MoU & Month 6 Milestone

During the initial 30-day pilot sprint (Project: First Frame), no equity is vested. Upon successful completion and transition into the long-term unit, equity is bound via our formal Pre-Incorporation MoU.

Official Private Limited incorporation is executed at Month 6—coinciding perfectly with our first vesting cliff.

  • PHASE 1: Project-Based Pilot (30 Days / Project: First Frame)
  • PHASE 2: 6-Month Cliff // 0% Earned before Month 6, 25% Unlocks at Month 6
  • PHASE 3: Official Private Limited Incorporation & Cap Table Issuance (Month 6)
  • PHASE 4: 18-Month Linear Monthly Vesting (Capping full allocation at Month 24)
03 // STUDIO SAFEGUARDS & MALPRACTICE PROTECTION

Operational Integrity & Buyback Clauses

To protect Rethinker Media from stagnation or bad-faith exits ("cliff raiding"), our agreements enforce strict, industry-standard protections:

[ A. INSTANT IP ASSIGNMENT ]

All raw footage, audio stems, project files, and rendered assets created during production belong 100% to Rethinker Media from the exact moment of capture, regardless of physical hardware used.

[ B. NOMINAL SHARE BUYBACK / CLAWBACK (ROFR) ]

If a contributor departs prior to full vesting, the studio holds the absolute legal right to buy back all unvested or vested shares for a nominal price (₹1), preventing inactive holders from occupying cap table space.

[ C. ACTIVE CONTRIBUTION REVENUE SPLITS ]

Any financial payouts (ad revenue splits, brand bonuses) are strictly tied to active, ongoing monthly production. The moment a contributor exits, cash flow shares drop to zero.
